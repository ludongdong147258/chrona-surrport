Chrona

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

The short version: Chrona stores everything on your device. We do not operate servers, we do not require an account, and we never see your focus sessions, thoughts, or routines. Your data is yours.

1.Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how Chrona ("the App"), developed by dongdonglu ("we", "us"), handles information when you use the App on your iPhone. Questions? Contact us at fishspotradar@163.com.

2.Information We Do Not Collect

Chrona is a fully local app. We do not collect, transmit, or store on any server:

  • Personal information — there is no account, sign-up, or login.
  • Usage analytics — the App contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • Tracking data — we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not sell or share data with data brokers.

3.Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data only on your device, in local app storage. It never leaves your iPhone unless you explicitly export or sync it (see Sections 4 and 5):

  • Timer settings — your selected focus duration and protocol.
  • Custom sensory protocols — routines you create in the craft-protocol builder.
  • Session history — records of completed focus sessions shown in the Insight tab.
  • Parked thoughts — entries you save to Thought Park.
  • Sound settings — your masking sound preferences.
  • Purchase state — whether Pro features are unlocked, so the App can restore your entitlement offline.

You can delete any of this at any time by deleting the items in the App, or delete everything at once by uninstalling the App.

4.Apple Health (Optional, Off by Default)

If you explicitly turn on Apple Health syncing, the App can write your completed focus sessions to the Apple Health app on your device. This is a one-way write:

  • The App does not read any health data.
  • Health data stays in the Apple Health ecosystem — it is never sent to us.
  • You can revoke access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Health.

5.Purchases

Chrona Pro is purchased through Apple's App Store using your Apple ID. We do not receive or store your name, payment card, or billing address.

  • Payment is processed entirely by Apple. See Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.
  • We use RevenueCat to verify purchases and manage entitlements. RevenueCat processes transaction identifiers and purchase metadata (such as product ID and expiry date) under its own privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

6.Live Activities

During a focus session, Chrona can show a Live Activity on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. Live Activities are rendered entirely on your device and are not transmitted anywhere.

7.Data Retention and Deletion

Because all App data lives on your device, uninstalling Chrona permanently deletes all of it. We retain nothing.

8.Children's Privacy

Chrona is a general-purpose focus tool and is not directed at children under 13. Since we collect no personal information from anyone, no personal information is collected from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it — although, as described above, we never receive App data in the first place.

9.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective date" at the top shows the latest revision. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
